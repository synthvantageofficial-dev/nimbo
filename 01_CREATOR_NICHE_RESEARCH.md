## 2. COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE (what exists today)

Today's market = **point tools** + a few **messy auto-pilot scripts**. Nobody owns the trusted, unified *agent* layer for real creators.

### 2a. Point tools (each does ONE slice; creator must operate them)
| Tool | Does | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **OpusClip / Choppity / Vizard / Klap** | long video → viral shorts | $12–29/mo |
| **vidIQ / TubeBuddy** | SEO, keywords, coaching | ~$16/mo |
| **Jasper / Copy.ai** | marketing copy, GTM workflows | $49/mo |
| **HeyGen** | script → finished AI video in <5 min | paid |
| **ElevenLabs** | voice clone, 29-language dubbing | paid |
| **Descript / Pictory / InVideo** | editing, faceless video | $16+/mo |
| **Canva AI** | thumbnails, graphics | paid |
| **Metricool / Buffer** | scheduling, analytics | paid |

**Weakness of all of them:** they are *features*, not an *agent*. The creator still has to decide, operate, stitch, and remember. No memory of *you*, no cross-tool orchestration, no "just tell me what you need and I'll handle it."

### 2b. Auto-pilot scripts (the "agent" attempts that exist)
- **Open-source projects** (e.g. "TUBEGENT" / youtube-automation-agent on GitHub): "6 AI agents, free Gemini, no-code, runs a channel 24/7." Real — but aimed at **faceless, fully-automated spam channels**, DIY setup, hobby-grade, no trust/brand/polish.
- **Creator experiments:** one person let 2 agents run a channel for 6 weeks → 52 videos, ~30k views, 29 subscribers, 4–5% like rate. I.e. **novelty-grade, mediocre output.**

### 2c. The market's own direction (tailwind for us)
The industry openly shifted **from "generation" (2023–24) to "operations" (2025–26)** — research, ideation, scoring, scheduling, optimization done *by agents*. We are riding that wave at the right moment, but aiming it at **real creators with a face/brand/voice**, not faceless autopilot.
